The school year,
on one boardNine stages, six service lines, and every route in between.

DigiSchool-Pro runs a whole school from one tenant boundary: a term is opened, classes and subjects are built, teachers mark and score, results are compiled and published, money is billed and reconciled, families read only what belongs to them, and the year rolls forward. Pick any stop to see its route, what it needs, and what breaks without it.

From opening the school to closing the term

Stages one and two must happen in order — nothing else is safe until a school exists and a term is current. Stages five to eight run side by side through the term. Stage nine hands the school back to stage two for the next term.

Six lines, one shared interchange

Every line carries its own work, but all of them stop at the same four interchanges: who you are, which school you are in, which term is current, and what was written down. Hover a line to watch it run.

What each module needs before it can work

Columns follow the term cycle, so dependencies read left to right. Select a station to light its upstream requirements in green and everything that would fall over downstream in orange.

Nine rules that hold the records together

How one request reaches the data
